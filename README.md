# Seasonal Zooplankton Dynamics and Environmental Drivers in the Sangu River Estuary

## **Project Overview**
This repository hosts the research data and analytical framework for the first comprehensive baseline study of zooplankton dynamics in the Sangu River estuary, Bangladesh. The research investigates how seasonal monsoon cycles and physicochemical gradients (salinity, nutrients, DO) shape the community structure of zooplankton across 12 geo-referenced sites.

## **Key Research Highlights**
*   **Study Period:** One-year cycle covering pre-monsoon, monsoon, and post-monsoon periods.
*   **Taxonomic Focus:** 10 major zooplankton groups, with a dominant focus on copepod abundance (up to 65%).
*   **Ecological Metrics:** Seasonal richness, diversity (Margalef’s and Shannon-Wiener indices), and community turnover.
*   **Statistical Analysis:** Advanced multivariate techniques including **NMDS**, **Mantel tests**, **RDA**, and **PCA** conducted in R.

---

## **Repository Structure**
*   **`/Data`**: Cleaned datasets containing seasonal abundance (ind./m³) and georeferenced environmental parameters.
*   **`/Scripts`**: R scripts used for:
    *   Diversity indices calculation.
    *   Multivariate community analysis (Hierarchical clustering & NMDS).
    *   Redundancy Analysis (RDA) and PCA for environmental drivers.
*   **`/Figures`**: High-resolution plots of seasonal community shifts and physicochemical gradients.
